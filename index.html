Unforgettable Characters

✦
JN

John Nolan

The Rookie

At 40, John Nolan leaves his old life behind and moves to LA to follow his dream of becoming a police officer. Faced with challenges at every turn — from skeptical colleagues to life-threatening situations — he proves that it's never too late to start over.

MU

Murphy

Z Nation

The last hope for humanity. After a zombie virus tears the world apart, Murphy is injected with an experimental antidote that may be the only thing standing between survival and extinction — making him the most important and complicated man alive.

H&G

Hannah Wells & Garrett Graham

Off Campus

A slow-burn, heart-tugging love story between a talented music student and the university's star hockey player. Two different worlds, one undeniable connection — and Hannah's voice ties it all together beautifully.

Z&L

Zhu Yun & Li Xun

Lighter & Princess

Zhu Yun's fresh university life is thrown into chaos the moment the brooding, ice-cold bad boy Li Xun arrives. Despite his walls and cold exterior, the pull between them is undeniable — even when he pushes her away to protect her.

ELK

Ekim, Leyla Pinar & Kanat Gunay

Hear Me

When Ekim's best friend Leyla is left injured in a hit-and-run, Ekim infiltrates the wealthy high school responsible to find justice. But her mission gets complicated when she falls for Kanat Gunay — the school's most feared bully — and her heart starts fighting her head.

JJ

Jasper "Jay-Jay" Mariano

The Jewel of Section E

All Jay wanted was a fresh start at a new school — instead she finds herself the only girl in the notorious all-male delinquent Section E. Targeted by pranks from every direction and caught in a chaotic love triangle, Jay-Jay proves she can hold her own against whatever the boys throw at her.
